# Regulatory Obligation Specification

## Feature

Students practice basic maths numeracy by speaking answers aloud. The application captures
voice audio via the device microphone, uses speech recognition to transcribe spoken responses,
provides immediate feedback, and logs all responses (including audio for replay) in a per-student
history that students can review.

## Applicable Provisions and Obligations

OB1: The processing of student voice audio, speech transcriptions, and response-history logs must be carried out on a lawful basis; the applicable legal basis (for example, explicit consent for audio capture and storage) must be identified and documented for each distinct processing activity.

OB2: Where consent is the identified legal basis, the controller must be able to demonstrate that each student (or, where required, their parent or guardian) gave consent that was freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous; the system must provide a mechanism to withdraw consent as easily as it was given.

OB3: Because the application is directed at students who may be below 16 years of age, where processing relies on consent, that consent must be given or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility for any student below the applicable age threshold (16 years, or a lower age set by the relevant Member State, not below 13); the controller must implement reasonable efforts to verify such authorisation before processing begins.

OB4: Voice audio captured via the device microphone constitutes biometric personal data; processing of such data is prohibited unless a specific exception applies, such as the explicit consent of the data subject or, where the student is a child, of the holder of parental responsibility.

OB5: Personal data collected (voice audio, transcriptions, response-history logs) must be collected only for the specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes of enabling numeracy practice, providing immediate feedback, and maintaining response history for student review; the data must not be further processed in a manner incompatible with those stated purposes.

OB6: Only personal data that is adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary for numeracy practice, feedback, and history review must be collected; in particular, raw audio recordings must not be retained if the transcription alone is sufficient to fulfil the stated purposes.

OB7: Student response history, transcriptions, and any retained audio recordings must be kept for no longer than is necessary for the stated purposes; response history and associated audio must be deleted or irreversibly anonymised at the end of the retention period. [retention: 12 months from the date of collection]

OB8: At the point of first use and before the microphone is activated, students and, where applicable, their parents or guardians must be provided with the following information: the purposes for which voice audio and response history are processed, the legal basis for that processing, the categories of recipients (if any), and the retention period. [retention: 12 months from the date of collection]

OB9: Students and, where applicable, their parents or guardians must be able to request erasure of the student's voice audio, transcriptions, and response-history logs without undue delay, in particular where consent is withdrawn or where the data are no longer necessary for the stated purposes.

OB10: Appropriate technical and organisational measures must be implemented to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk when storing and transmitting voice audio, transcriptions, and response-history logs, taking into account the sensitivity of biometric data and the likelihood that data subjects are children.
