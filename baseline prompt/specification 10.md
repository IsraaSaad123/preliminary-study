# Regulatory Obligation Specification

## Feature: Maths Numeracy Practice with Speech Recognition

### Processing Activities

This feature collects and processes the following personal data:

- Voice recordings captured via the device microphone
- Speech-to-text transcriptions of spoken answers
- Session history logs associating responses with individual students (for replay and review)

Students are likely to be minors. Voice recordings stored for replay may constitute biometric data. All three categories of data are linked to identifiable individuals.

### Applicable Provision Codes

OB1, OB2, OB3, OB4, OB5, OB6, OB7, OB8, OB9, OB10

### Obligations

OB1: The controller must identify and document a valid lawful basis under Art. 6 for each distinct processing activity — capturing voice recordings via the microphone, transcribing spoken responses, and retaining session history logs — before any processing commences.

OB2: Where consent is selected as the lawful basis, the controller must obtain and be able to demonstrate freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent from each student, and must provide a mechanism to withdraw consent that is as easy as the mechanism used to give it.

OB3: Because the application is directed at students who may be under 16, where processing is based on consent the controller must verify each student's age; for students below 16 (or the applicable Member State threshold, not below 13), consent must be given or authorised by a holder of parental responsibility, and the controller must make reasonable efforts to verify that authorisation.

OB4: Voice recordings captured via the microphone and retained in session history may constitute biometric data; the controller must establish that an explicit exception under Art. 9(2) applies — such as the data subject's explicit consent — before capturing or storing audio recordings.

OB5: Voice recordings, transcriptions, and session history logs must be collected solely for the specified purpose of supporting student maths numeracy practice and review, and must not be processed in any manner incompatible with that purpose.

OB6: The controller must limit data collection to what is adequate, relevant, and strictly necessary; in particular, it must assess whether raw audio recordings need to be retained once a transcription has been produced and whether full session logs are necessary for each student interaction.

OB7: Voice recordings, transcriptions, and session history logs must not be kept in a form that permits identification of students for longer than is necessary for the educational purpose. [retention: 12 months from the date of collection, or upon termination of the student's active enrolment, whichever is the earlier]

OB8: At the point of first microphone activation, the controller must provide each student (and, where required under OB3, a parent or guardian) with the purposes of processing, the lawful basis, any recipients of the data, and the retention period. [retention: 12 months from the date of collection, or upon termination of the student's active enrolment, whichever is the earlier]

OB9: The controller must provide each student with a mechanism to request erasure of their voice recordings, transcriptions, and session history logs without undue delay where the data are no longer necessary, consent is withdrawn, or no other lawful basis for continued retention exists.

OB10: The controller must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk of processing voice recordings, transcriptions, and session history logs, taking into account the sensitivity of voice data and the likelihood that data subjects are minors.
