# Regulatory Obligation Specification

**Feature:** Maths Numeracy Practice with Microphone and Response History  
**Derived from:** gdpr_provisions.md (OB1–OB10)

---

OB1: The application must identify and document a lawful legal basis before processing any student voice audio, speech-recognition transcriptions, or logged response history; consent is the identified legal basis for this feature.

OB2: Consent must be obtained in a freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous form before microphone access is activated or any response data is recorded; the mechanism for withdrawing consent must be as simple and accessible as the mechanism for giving it.

OB3: Because the application is directed at students who may be under 16, consent for processing voice and response data is valid only where the student is 16 or older; for students below that age, consent must be given or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility, and the application must make reasonable efforts to verify that authorisation before any data are collected.

OB4: Voice audio captured via the device microphone constitutes biometric-capable personal data under Art. 9; processing is prohibited unless explicit consent (or another applicable Art. 9 exception) has been obtained separately from the Art. 6 legal basis, and this explicit consent must be recorded before the microphone is accessed.

OB5: Voice audio, speech-recognition transcriptions, and logged response history must be collected and processed solely for the specified purpose of supporting student maths numeracy practice and review; no further processing incompatible with that purpose (for example, voice profiling or advertising analytics) is permitted.

OB6: Data collection must be limited to what is strictly necessary for the educational purpose: raw audio recordings should be deleted immediately after transcription, and only the transcribed response text, question identifiers, timestamps, and session identifiers need to be retained in the response history.

OB7: Raw voice audio must be deleted as soon as transcription processing is complete and no later than 7 days after the recording session; transcribed response history and session logs must be deleted no later than 12 months from the date of collection. [retention: raw audio — 7 days post-session; transcribed response history — 12 months from date of collection]

OB8: At the point of first data collection (before microphone activation), the application must present students and, where applicable, their parents or guardians with the following information: the purposes of processing (maths practice and response review), the legal basis (consent), any recipients of the data, and the applicable retention periods (raw audio deleted within 7 days; response history deleted after 12 months). [retention: raw audio — 7 days post-session; transcribed response history — 12 months from date of collection]

OB9: Students and, where applicable, their parents or guardians must be able to request erasure of all stored response history, session logs, and any retained audio without undue delay; the application must provide a clear mechanism to exercise this right, and erasure must be carried out promptly upon request where no overriding legal obligation requires continued retention.

OB10: The application must implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures for voice audio, transcriptions, and response history, including encryption of data in transit and at rest, access controls limiting who can view stored student records, and measures commensurate with the heightened sensitivity of processing voice data from children.
