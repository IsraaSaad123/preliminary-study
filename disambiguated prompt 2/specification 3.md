# Regulatory Obligation Specification
## Feature: Voice-Based Maths Numeracy Practice

### Feature Summary

Students practice basic maths numeracy by speaking answers aloud into their device microphone. The application uses speech recognition to transcribe spoken responses and provide immediate feedback. Voice audio is processed solely to recognise the spoken numerical content of each answer; it is not used to identify or distinguish individual speakers. All student responses are logged in app history so that students can review previous attempts and replay lessons.

### Applicable Provisions and Obligations

OB1: The application must identify and document a lawful basis under Art. 6 for each processing activity: (a) capture and processing of voice audio to extract spoken numerical answers, (b) transcription of spoken responses, and (c) retention of response history linked to the student. [retention: N/A]

OB2: Where the lawful basis for any processing activity is consent, the controller must obtain freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent from the student (or their parent/guardian where applicable) before that activity commences, must be able to demonstrate that consent was obtained, and must provide a mechanism to withdraw consent as easily as it was given. [retention: N/A]

OB3: Because the application is an information society service offered directly to students who are likely to include children under the age of 16, and where processing relies on consent, the application must verify the student's age; for students below the applicable age threshold (16, or a lower Member State limit of not below 13), consent must be given or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility, and the controller must make reasonable efforts to verify that authorisation before processing commences. [retention: N/A]

OB4: Although voice audio is processed, the application explicitly does not process that audio to uniquely identify or distinguish individual speakers. Should any future change in design cause voice audio to be processed for the purpose of uniquely identifying a natural person (rendering it biometric data within the meaning of Art. 9), processing would be prohibited unless a specific Art. 9 exception applies; the controller must keep this classification under review and must not repurpose the audio for identification without first satisfying Art. 9. [retention: N/A]

OB5: The purposes for which personal data are processed must be specified, explicit, and legitimate before collection begins. The defined purposes are: (a) real-time speech recognition to evaluate a student's spoken numerical answer and deliver immediate feedback; (b) storage of transcribed responses in the student's app history to enable self-review of previous attempts and replay of lessons. Personal data must not be further processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes. [retention: N/A]

OB6: Personal data collected and retained must be limited to what is necessary for the stated purposes. Voice audio must be discarded immediately after the numerical content has been extracted and must not be stored. Logged history entries must contain only the information required for self-review (for example, the question, the recognised numerical answer, the result, and a timestamp); no additional acoustic, behavioural, or identifying data may be retained. [retention: N/A]

OB7: Student response history stored in the application must be kept for no longer than is necessary for the purpose of enabling self-review of previous attempts and replay of lessons. Response history must be deleted no later than 12 months from the date each entry was recorded, or upon deletion of the student's account, or upon the student's completion of or withdrawal from the course, whichever occurs first. [retention: 12 months from the date of recording, or upon account deletion or course completion/withdrawal, whichever is earliest]

OB8: At or before the point at which a student's voice data and response history are first collected, the controller must provide the student (and, where OB3 applies, the parent or guardian) with the following information: (a) the identity and contact details of the controller; (b) the purposes of processing and the legal basis for each activity; (c) the categories of personal data collected (voice audio for real-time recognition; transcribed responses and history entries); (d) any recipients or categories of recipients of the data (for example, third-party speech recognition service providers); (e) the retention period of 12 months from the date of recording, or until account deletion or course completion/withdrawal, whichever is earliest; (f) the existence of data subject rights including access, rectification, erasure, and the right to withdraw consent. [retention: 12 months from the date of recording, or upon account deletion or course completion/withdrawal, whichever is earliest]

OB9: The application must provide students (and, where applicable, their parent or guardian) with a mechanism to request erasure of their stored response history without undue delay, in particular where the data are no longer necessary for their purpose, where consent is withdrawn and no other legal basis applies, or where the student raises a valid objection. The erasure mechanism must be accessible within the application. [retention: N/A]

OB10: The controller must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the personal data processed by this feature, taking into account the sensitivity of the data and the likely users (children). Required measures include: encryption of stored response history in transit and at rest; access controls that restrict history data to the individual student and authorised personnel; automatic deletion mechanisms to enforce the retention period defined under OB7; and, where voice audio is transmitted to a third-party speech recognition service, contractual safeguards ensuring that service processes the audio solely for transcription and does not retain or repurpose it. [retention: N/A]
