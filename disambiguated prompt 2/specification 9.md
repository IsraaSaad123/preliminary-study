# Regulatory Obligation Specification

## Feature

Students practice basic maths numeracy by speaking answers aloud into their device microphone. A speech-recognition component transcribes the spoken numerical response and provides immediate feedback. Voice audio is processed solely to recognise the spoken numerical content; it is not used to identify or distinguish individual speakers. All student responses are logged in an in-app history so that students can review previous attempts and replay lessons.

## Applicable Provisions and Obligations

OB1: The controller must identify and document a lawful legal basis under Art. 6 for each processing activity: (a) real-time processing of voice audio for speech recognition and (b) logging and retention of student response history for lesson review. [retention: not applicable]

OB2: Where processing relies on consent, the controller must be able to demonstrate that each student (or their parent or guardian where OB3 applies) consented; consent must be freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous, and the controller must provide a mechanism to withdraw consent that is as easy to use as the mechanism to give it. [retention: not applicable]

OB3: Because the service is directed at students who may be under 16, where processing relies on consent the controller must verify each student's age and, for students below the applicable national threshold (16 years, or as low as 13 where a Member State has so provided), must obtain consent given or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility, and must make reasonable efforts to verify that authorisation. [retention: not applicable]

OB5: The controller must define the purpose of processing as supporting mathematical numeracy practice and delivering immediate performance feedback, and must not further process voice audio or response logs in a manner incompatible with that stated purpose. [retention: not applicable]

OB6: The controller must limit data collection to what is necessary for the stated purpose: voice audio must be used only to extract the spoken numerical response and must not be retained beyond the duration of that processing step; logged response history must contain only the data fields necessary to enable student review of previous attempts. [retention: not applicable]

OB7: The controller must define and enforce a retention period for student response logs; response logs must be deleted no later than 12 months from the date on which each response was recorded. [retention: 12 months from date of collection]

OB8: At the point of data collection the controller must provide students (and, where required by OB3, their parent or guardian) with the following information: the purposes of processing, the legal basis relied upon, any recipients of the data, and the retention period of 12 months. [retention: 12 months from date of collection]

OB9: The controller must implement a mechanism by which students (or, where applicable, their parent or guardian) can request erasure of the student's response history, and must delete that data without undue delay where no overriding legal basis for continued processing exists. [retention: not applicable]

OB10: The controller must implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect voice audio during processing and stored response logs against unauthorised access, accidental loss, and unlawful disclosure, proportionate to the risk posed by processing data relating to student data subjects. [retention: not applicable]

## Provision Codes That Apply

OB1, OB2, OB3, OB5, OB6, OB7, OB8, OB9, OB10

## Provision Code That Does Not Apply

OB4 — Voice audio is processed solely to recognise spoken numerical content and is not processed to uniquely identify or distinguish individual speakers; accordingly it does not constitute biometric data within the meaning of Art. 9, and the prohibition on processing special categories of personal data is not engaged.
