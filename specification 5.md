# Regulatory Obligation Specification

## Feature: Microphone-based Maths Numeracy Practice with Response History

The following obligations derive solely from the data protection provisions in `gdpr_provisions.md`.
Each obligation is expressed against the provision code to which it corresponds.

---

OB1: The controller must identify and document a lawful legal basis under Art. 6 for each distinct processing activity performed by the feature — capture of spoken responses via the device microphone, speech-to-text transcription, provision of immediate feedback, and persistent storage of student response history.

OB2: Where the lawful basis for any processing activity is consent, the controller must be able to demonstrate that each student's consent was freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous, and must ensure that withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.

OB3: Because the feature is directed at students who are likely to be children, where processing relies on consent the controller must accept consent only from students aged 16 or above; for students below that age, consent must be given or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility, and the controller must make reasonable efforts to verify such authorisation before any microphone data are captured or response history is stored.

OB4: Capture of spoken audio via the device microphone constitutes processing of biometric data capable of identifying a natural person; such processing is prohibited unless a specific exception under Art. 9(2) applies, such as the explicit consent of the data subject or, where the student is a child below the applicable age threshold, the explicit consent of the holder of parental responsibility.

OB5: The purposes for processing microphone audio, transcribed answers, and response-history records must be specified before collection begins; data collected for maths-practice feedback and lesson-history review must not be further processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes.

OB6: Only the personal data strictly necessary for providing speech-based feedback and maintaining response history may be collected; where a transcribed text answer is sufficient to deliver feedback and populate the history log, raw audio must not be retained beyond what is required to complete transcription.

OB7: Response-history records — including transcribed answers, associated feedback, and any retained audio — must be deleted or anonymised once they are no longer necessary for the educational purpose. [retention: 12 months from the date of collection, or on account closure or withdrawal of consent, whichever is earliest]

OB8: At the point at which microphone audio and response data are first collected, the controller must provide each student — and, where the student is a child below the applicable age threshold, their parent or guardian — with the identity of the controller, the purposes of processing, the lawful basis, any recipients of the data, and the applicable retention period. [retention: 12 months from the date of collection, or on account closure or withdrawal of consent, whichever is earliest]

OB9: Students — or their parents or guardians where the student is a child — must be able to request erasure of all personal data held by the feature, including microphone recordings, transcribed answers, and response-history logs, without undue delay, and the controller must honour such requests where the data are no longer necessary for the stated purposes or where consent is withdrawn.

OB10: The controller must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect microphone audio, transcriptions, and response-history logs against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or loss, taking into account the elevated risk arising from the fact that the data subjects are children.
