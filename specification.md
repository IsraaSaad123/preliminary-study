# Regulatory Obligation Specification

## Feature

Students practice basic maths numeracy by speaking answers aloud. The device microphone captures audio, which is processed transiently by speech recognition to produce a text transcript. Only the transcribed text is stored in the app history; raw audio is not retained. Students may review previous attempts and replay lessons using the stored transcripts.

## Applicable Provisions and Obligations

OB1: The controller must identify and document a valid legal basis under Art. 6 for storing transcribed student responses (e.g., consent or legitimate interest in delivering the educational service); no processing of the transcribed text may occur without an identified basis. [retention: N/A]

OB2: Where consent is relied upon as the legal basis, the controller must obtain freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent from each student before microphone access and storage of transcribed responses begin, and must make withdrawal of consent as easy as giving it, ceasing storage and offering erasure upon withdrawal. [retention: N/A]

OB3: Because the service is directed at students who may be under 16, where processing is based on consent the controller must verify the student's age and, for students below 16, obtain consent given or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility, making reasonable efforts to verify that authorisation before any data are collected. [retention: N/A]

OB5: The controller must define a specified, explicit, and legitimate purpose for storing transcribed responses — namely enabling students to review previous attempts and replay lessons — and must not process the transcribed data in a manner incompatible with that stated purpose. [retention: N/A]

OB6: The controller must ensure that only the transcribed text of each response is stored; raw audio must not be retained beyond the transient duration required for transcription, and no additional data fields beyond those necessary to associate a transcript with a student's lesson history may be stored. [retention: N/A]

OB7: The controller must define and enforce a retention period for stored transcribed responses, keeping them only for as long as necessary for students to review previous attempts; transcribed responses must be deleted no later than 12 months after the end of the student's active enrolment or account closure, whichever is earlier. [retention: 12 months after end of active enrolment or account closure, whichever is earlier]

OB8: At the point of collecting microphone input, the controller must provide each student (and, where OB3 applies, their parent or guardian) with the following information: the purposes for which transcribed responses are stored, the legal basis for that storage, any recipients or categories of recipients, and the retention period as specified under OB7. [retention: 12 months after end of active enrolment or account closure, whichever is earlier]

OB9: The controller must provide a mechanism by which students, or their parents or guardians where applicable, can request erasure of stored transcribed responses, and must act on such requests without undue delay where the data are no longer necessary for the stated purpose or where consent is withdrawn and no other legal basis applies. [retention: N/A]

OB10: The controller must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to secure stored transcribed responses against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or loss, taking into account that the data subjects are children and that the audio channel during transient processing also requires protection in transit. [retention: N/A]

## Provision Codes Applied

OB1, OB2, OB3, OB5, OB6, OB7, OB8, OB9, OB10

## Provision Codes Not Applied

OB4 — Art. 9 applies to biometric data processed for the purpose of uniquely identifying a natural person. The audio captured here is processed transiently for speech-to-text transcription (extraction of linguistic content), not for the purpose of uniquely identifying the student. The data stored — transcribed text — is not biometric data. OB4 therefore does not apply.
