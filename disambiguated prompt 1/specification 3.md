# Regulatory Obligation Specification
## Feature: Spoken Maths Numeracy Practice with Speech Recognition

### Feature Summary

Students speak answers to maths questions aloud. The application captures audio transiently via the device microphone, uses speech recognition to produce a text transcription, and immediately discards the raw audio. Only the transcribed text of each response is persisted in the student's app history, enabling review of previous attempts and replay of lessons.

---

### Applicable Provisions and Obligations

OB1: The application must identify and document a valid legal basis under Art. 6 for each distinct processing activity: (a) transient processing of raw audio for speech recognition, and (b) storage of transcribed text responses in the student's history. [retention: n/a]

OB2: Where the legal basis for any processing activity is consent, the application must obtain freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent from the student (or their parent/guardian where OB3 applies) before audio capture begins, and must provide an equally simple mechanism to withdraw that consent at any time. [retention: n/a]

OB3: Because the application is an information society service directed at students who may be under 16, where processing relies on consent the application must verify that users aged under 16 have consent given or authorised by a holder of parental responsibility, and must make reasonable efforts to verify that authorisation before processing commences. [retention: n/a]

OB5: The transcribed text collected and stored in the student's history must be used only for the specified purposes of providing immediate feedback and enabling the student to review previous attempts and replay lessons; it must not be further processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes. [retention: n/a]

OB6: Only the transcribed text of each response may be stored; raw audio must not be retained beyond the transient processing required for transcription. The transcribed text itself must be limited to what is necessary for feedback and lesson review and must not include extraneous personal data. [retention: n/a]

OB7: Transcribed response records must be kept for no longer than is necessary for their purpose. The retention period must be defined as: transcribed responses are retained for a maximum of 12 months from the date of each attempt, or until the student's account is closed or deleted, whichever is sooner, after which they must be securely deleted or anonymised. [retention: 12 months from the date of each attempt, or until account closure, whichever is sooner]

OB8: At the point of first data collection (before the microphone is first activated), the application must present the student (and, where OB3 applies, their parent or guardian) with clear information covering: the purposes of processing (feedback and lesson history review), the legal basis relied upon, any recipients or categories of recipients of the data, and the retention period as defined under OB7. [retention: 12 months from the date of each attempt, or until account closure, whichever is sooner]

OB9: The application must provide a mechanism by which a student (or their parent/guardian acting on their behalf) can request erasure of some or all stored transcribed responses, and must fulfil such requests without undue delay where no overriding legal basis for continued retention exists. [retention: n/a]

OB10: The application must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect stored transcribed responses against unauthorised access, accidental loss, or destruction, taking into account the nature of the data (identifiable student response records), the processing context (educational service involving minors), and the associated risk. [retention: n/a]
