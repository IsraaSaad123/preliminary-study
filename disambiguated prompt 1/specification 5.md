# Regulatory Obligation Specification

## Feature: Voice-Based Maths Numeracy Practice

### Processing Activity Summary

Students speak aloud answers to maths questions. Raw audio is captured by the device microphone and processed transiently by speech recognition to produce a transcription; the raw audio is not stored. The transcribed text of each response is stored in the app's history, linked to the student, so that students can review previous attempts and replay lessons.

### Applicable Provisions and Obligations

OB1: The app must identify and document a valid legal basis (for example, consent or legitimate interest of the educational institution) for each processing activity — specifically, the transient processing of raw audio for transcription and the storage of transcribed text in the student's history. [retention: N/A]

OB2: Where the legal basis for processing is consent, the controller must obtain consent that is freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous — in particular for microphone access and for storing transcribed responses — and must make withdrawal of consent as easy as granting it. [retention: N/A]

OB3: Because the app is directed at students who may be under 16, and where processing relies on consent, the controller must verify that consent is given or authorised by a holder of parental responsibility for any student below the applicable age threshold (16, or a lower Member State limit of not below 13). [retention: N/A]

OB5: The transcribed text must be collected only for the specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes of providing feedback and enabling review of previous attempts; it must not be further processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes (for example, it must not be used for profiling or commercial purposes). [retention: N/A]

OB6: Only the transcribed text necessary for the educational feedback and review purposes may be stored; raw audio must not be retained beyond the transient processing needed for transcription, consistent with the stated design. [retention: N/A]

OB7: Transcribed response records stored in the student's history must not be kept longer than is necessary for the purpose of reviewing previous attempts. [retention: 12 months from the date each response is recorded, after which records must be deleted or anonymised]

OB8: At the point of first collection (for example, when the feature is first enabled for a student), the controller must provide the student and, where the student is a child requiring parental authorisation, the parent or guardian, with the purposes of processing, the legal basis, any recipients of the data, and the retention period. [retention: 12 months from the date each response is recorded]

OB9: The controller must provide a mechanism for students (and, where applicable, parents or guardians) to request erasure of stored transcribed response history without undue delay, including when consent is withdrawn or when the data are no longer necessary for their purpose. [retention: N/A]

OB10: The controller must implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures covering both the transient processing of raw audio and the stored transcribed text, proportionate to the risks posed by processing personal data of students. [retention: N/A]

### Provisions Not Applicable

**OB4** (special categories / biometric data, Art. 9) does not apply: raw audio is processed transiently solely for speech-to-text transcription and is not stored; the purpose of processing the voice input is transcription of a spoken number, not unique identification of the person. The data stored (transcribed text of maths answers) is not a special category.
