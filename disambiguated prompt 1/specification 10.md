# Regulatory Obligation Specification

## Feature
Maths numeracy practice using device microphone. Students speak answers aloud; speech recognition transcribes responses transiently and only the transcribed text is stored in app history for review and lesson replay. Raw audio is not retained.

## Applicable Provisions and Obligations

OB1: The system must identify and document a lawful basis for processing students' transcribed responses before any processing commences; the applicable basis must be recorded for each processing activity (transient audio capture and storage of transcribed text).

OB2: Where consent is relied upon as the lawful basis, the system must obtain freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent from the data subject, and must provide a mechanism to withdraw consent that is as easy to use as the mechanism to give it.

OB3: Where consent is the lawful basis and a student is below the applicable age threshold (16 years, or 13 years where the relevant Member State has exercised that derogation), consent must be given or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility; the system must make reasonable efforts to verify that such authorisation has been provided before processing begins.

OB4: The transient capture and processing of voice audio from the microphone constitutes processing of biometric data; such processing is prohibited unless a specific exception applies, such as the explicit consent of the data subject or, where the student is below the applicable age threshold, the explicit consent of the holder of parental responsibility.

OB5: Transcribed responses must be collected solely for the specified purpose of enabling students to review their practice attempts and replay lessons; they must not be further processed in a manner incompatible with that purpose.

OB6: Only the transcribed text of each spoken response shall be stored; raw audio must not be retained beyond the transient processing required for transcription; no additional personal data beyond what is strictly necessary for the review and replay purpose shall be collected or stored.

OB7: Transcribed responses must be deleted upon the end of the student's active enrolment period, and in any event no later than 12 months after the student's last recorded activity on the system, whichever is earlier. [retention: duration of active enrolment, maximum 12 months after last recorded activity]

OB8: At the point of data collection, the system must provide students and, where the student is below the applicable age threshold, their parent or guardian, with the identity of the controller, the purposes of processing, the lawful basis, any recipients of the data, and the applicable retention period. [retention: duration of active enrolment, maximum 12 months after last recorded activity]

OB9: The system must provide a mechanism by which students, or their parents or guardians where applicable, can request erasure of stored transcribed responses, and must act on such requests without undue delay where no overriding legal basis for continued retention exists.

OB10: Appropriate technical and organisational security measures must be implemented for both the transient processing of raw audio and the persistent storage of transcribed responses, commensurate with the risk arising from processing personal data of children.
