# Regulatory Obligation Specification

## Feature
Speech-based mathematics numeracy practice: students speak answers aloud; the application
transcribes audio via speech recognition and stores only the resulting transcribed text in
app history. Raw audio is processed transiently and not retained.

## Applicable Provisions and Obligations

OB1: The controller must identify and document a lawful basis under Art. 6 for each
processing activity in this feature — specifically (a) the transient processing of voice
audio for speech-to-text transcription, and (b) the ongoing storage of transcribed
responses in app history. [retention: n/a]

OB2: Where processing of transcribed responses relies on consent as its legal basis, the
controller must obtain consent that is freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous,
must be able to demonstrate that consent was given, and must ensure that withdrawal of
consent is as easy as giving it. [retention: n/a]

OB3: Because this service is offered directly to children (students of basic numeracy) and
constitutes an information society service, consent for processing is only lawful where the
child is at least 16 (or the applicable national minimum, no lower than 13); for children
below that age, consent must be given or authorised by the holder of parental
responsibility, and the controller must make reasonable efforts to verify that
authorisation. [retention: n/a]

OB4: Voice audio, even when processed only transiently, may constitute biometric data
within the meaning of Art. 9(1). The controller must assess whether the speech-recognition
processing falls within the definition of biometric data processed to uniquely identify a
natural person; if it does, processing is prohibited unless a specific exception under
Art. 9(2) applies (for example, explicit consent), and that exception must be identified
and documented. [retention: n/a]

OB5: The transcribed text stored in app history must be collected and used only for the
specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes for which it was collected — namely enabling
students to review previous practice attempts and replay lessons — and must not be further
processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes. [retention: n/a]

OB6: The controller must ensure that only the transcribed text necessary for the identified
purposes is stored; raw audio must not be retained, and no additional personal data beyond
what is strictly necessary for practice review and replay must be collected or stored.
[retention: n/a]

OB7: Transcribed responses stored in app history must be kept for no longer than is
necessary for the purpose of enabling practice review and lesson replay; the retention
period is defined as the duration of the student's active account, with all transcription
data deleted no later than 12 months after the account becomes inactive or is closed.
[retention: duration of active account + 12 months after account becomes inactive or is
closed]

OB8: At the point of collecting voice input and transcribed responses, the controller must
provide the student (and, where the student is a child below the applicable consent age,
the holder of parental responsibility) with the following information: the identity of the
controller, the purposes of processing (transcription for immediate feedback and storage of
transcribed text for practice review), the legal basis for processing, any recipients of
the data (including any third-party speech-recognition service), and the retention period
(duration of active account plus 12 months after account becomes inactive or is closed).
[retention: duration of active account + 12 months after account becomes inactive or is
closed]

OB9: The controller must provide students (and, where applicable, holders of parental
responsibility) with a mechanism to request erasure of stored transcribed responses without
undue delay, and must fulfil such requests where the data are no longer necessary for the
stated purposes or where consent is withdrawn and no other lawful basis exists. [retention: n/a]

OB10: The controller must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to
secure both the transient processing of voice audio (including any transmission to a
speech-recognition service) and the stored transcribed responses against unauthorised
access, disclosure, or loss, commensurate with the risk and the fact that the data subjects
are children. [retention: n/a]
