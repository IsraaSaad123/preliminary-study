# Regulatory Obligation Specification

## Feature
Maths numeracy practice via microphone: students speak answers aloud; speech recognition transcribes responses transiently; only the transcribed text is persisted in app history for review.

## Applicable Provisions and Obligations

OB1: The controller must identify and document a lawful basis under Art. 6 for processing the transcribed text of students' spoken answers before that processing begins. [retention: N/A]

OB2: Where the identified legal basis is consent, the controller must obtain freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent from each student (or their authorised representative) before microphone access and transcription commence, and must ensure withdrawal of consent is as easy to effect as giving it. [retention: N/A]

OB3: Because the service is offered directly to students who may be under 16, where processing is based on consent the controller must verify whether each student has reached the applicable age of digital consent (16, or the lower Member State threshold, but not below 13); for students below that age, consent must be given or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility, and the controller must make reasonable efforts to verify that authorisation. [retention: N/A]

OB5: The transcribed text must be collected solely for the specified, explicit, and legitimate purpose of supporting the student's numeracy practice and enabling review of previous attempts, and must not be further processed in a manner incompatible with that purpose. [retention: N/A]

OB6: Only the transcribed text of each response may be stored; raw audio must not be retained beyond the transient processing required for transcription, and no additional personal data beyond what is strictly necessary for the practice and review purpose may be collected. [retention: N/A]

OB7: Transcribed response records must be kept in a form that permits identification of the student for no longer than is necessary for the practice and review purpose; the retention period is 12 months from the date of each response, or until the student's account is deactivated, whichever is sooner, after which records must be deleted or irreversibly anonymised. [retention: 12 months from date of response, or until account deactivation, whichever is sooner]

OB8: At the point of collection of transcribed responses, the controller must provide each student (and, where required, their parent or guardian) with the following information: the identity and contact details of the controller; the purposes of processing and the legal basis; any recipients of the data; and the retention period of 12 months from the date of each response or until account deactivation, whichever is sooner. [retention: 12 months from date of response, or until account deactivation, whichever is sooner]

OB9: The controller must provide students (and, where applicable, their parents or guardians) with a mechanism to request erasure of all stored transcribed responses without undue delay, in particular where consent is withdrawn and no other lawful basis for retention applies, or where the data are no longer necessary for the practice and review purpose. [retention: N/A]

OB10: The controller must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the stored transcribed responses against unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss, taking into account the sensitivity of the data, the involvement of children, and the risks arising from microphone-based input and network transmission. [retention: N/A]

## Provisions Not Applicable

OB4 (Art. 9 — special categories): Raw voice audio is processed transiently solely for speech-to-text transcription and is not stored; the processing is not carried out for the purpose of uniquely identifying a natural person. The stored transcribed text (students' spoken math answers) does not constitute biometric data or any other special category. OB4 therefore does not apply.
