# Regulatory Obligation Specification

## Feature
Basic maths numeracy practice with microphone input. Students speak answers aloud; the
application transcribes the spoken response using speech recognition, provides immediate
feedback, and stores only the transcribed text in the app history so students can review
previous attempts and replay lessons. Raw audio is processed transiently for transcription
and is not stored.

## Applicable Provisions and Obligations

OB1: The controller must identify and document a lawful basis under Art. 6 GDPR for each
processing activity, specifically: (a) the transient processing of raw voice audio for
speech-to-text transcription, and (b) the ongoing storage of transcribed spoken responses
in the student's app history.

OB2: Where processing of students' transcribed responses relies on consent as the lawful
basis, the controller must obtain freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent
from each student; must be able to demonstrate that consent was given; and must ensure that
withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.

OB3: Because the service is offered directly to students who may be under 16, where
processing relies on consent the controller must not rely solely on the student's own
consent for any student below the applicable age threshold; consent must instead be given
or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility, and the controller must make
reasonable efforts to verify that such authorisation has been obtained before any processing
commences.

OB5: Transcribed spoken responses stored in the app history must be collected and processed
only for the specified, explicit, and legitimate purpose of enabling students to review
their previous attempts and replay lessons; they must not be further processed in any
manner incompatible with that purpose.

OB6: Only the transcribed text of each spoken response may be stored; raw audio must not be
retained beyond what is necessary for transient transcription processing, and no personal
data beyond that which is strictly necessary for enabling review of previous attempts and
replay of lessons may be collected or retained.

OB7: Transcribed spoken responses stored in the app history must not be kept in a form that
permits identification of students for longer than is necessary for the purpose of enabling
them to review previous attempts and replay lessons; a defined retention period must be
applied and communicated. [retention: 12 months from the date of recording]

OB8: At the point at which students' spoken responses are first collected, the controller
must provide students and, where applicable, their parents or guardians with the following
information: the identity of the controller, the purposes for which the transcribed
responses are processed, the legal basis for that processing, any recipients of the data,
and the retention period. [retention: 12 months from the date of recording]

OB9: Students and, where applicable, their parents or guardians must be able to exercise
the right to erasure of stored transcribed responses without undue delay in circumstances
including where the data are no longer necessary for the purpose for which they were
collected or where consent is withdrawn and no other lawful basis applies; the controller
must provide a mechanism to action such requests.

OB10: The controller must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to
ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk presented by storing transcribed spoken
responses belonging to students who may be children, including measures to protect against
unauthorised access, accidental loss, and unlawful disclosure of the stored history data.
