# Regulatory Obligation Specification — Maths Numeracy Speech Feature

## Feature Summary

Students practise basic maths numeracy by speaking answers aloud. The application
captures microphone audio transiently, transcribes it via speech recognition, provides
immediate feedback, and stores only the resulting transcribed text in the student's app
history so that previous attempts can be reviewed. Raw audio is not retained.

## Applicable Provisions and Obligations

OB1: The application must identify and document a lawful basis under Art. 6 for storing
transcribed student responses (e.g., consent, or legitimate interests in delivering the
educational service); no transcribed text may be processed without that basis in place.
[retention: n/a]

OB2: Where the legal basis for storing transcribed responses is consent, the controller
must obtain freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent before microphone
access and transcription storage begin, must keep a record demonstrating that consent was
given, and must provide a mechanism to withdraw consent that is as easy to use as the
mechanism to give it. [retention: n/a]

OB3: Because the feature is offered directly to students who may be under 16, where
processing relies on consent the controller must make reasonable efforts to verify the
age of each student; for any student below the applicable age threshold (16, or the
Member State minimum of not below 13), consent or authorisation must be given by the
holder of parental responsibility before the student may use the microphone or have
responses stored. [retention: n/a]

OB5: The transcribed text of student responses must be collected solely for the specified
purposes of supporting maths numeracy practice and enabling review of previous attempts,
and must not be further processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes.
[retention: n/a]

OB6: Only the transcribed text of each response may be stored; raw audio must not be
retained. No additional personal data beyond what is necessary for the practice and
review purposes may be collected or stored in connection with this feature. [retention: n/a]

OB7: Transcribed student responses must be kept for no longer than is necessary for the
practice and review purposes; the retention period is defined as the duration of the
student's active account, plus a maximum of 12 months after the account becomes inactive
or is closed, at which point transcribed responses must be deleted or irreversibly
anonymised. [retention: duration of active account + 12 months after account inactivity or closure]

OB8: At the point of first use of the microphone feature, the controller must provide
each student (and, where OB3 applies, the holder of parental responsibility) with the
following information: the identity of the controller, the purposes and legal basis for
processing transcribed responses, that raw audio is not stored, the retention period
(duration of active account plus 12 months after account inactivity or closure), the
student's rights (including the right to erasure), and whether transcriptions are shared
with any recipients. [retention: duration of active account + 12 months after account inactivity or closure]

OB9: The controller must provide students (and, where applicable, holders of parental
responsibility) with the ability to request erasure of stored transcribed responses
without undue delay, and must fulfil such requests in the circumstances set out in
Art. 17, including where the data are no longer necessary for the purposes or where
consent is withdrawn and no other legal basis applies. [retention: n/a]

OB10: The controller must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to
secure transcribed student responses against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure,
taking into account the nature of the data (identifiable educational responses linked to
students who may be minors) and the risk; measures must also ensure that transient audio
data processed during transcription is not exposed or retained beyond the transcription
operation. [retention: n/a]
