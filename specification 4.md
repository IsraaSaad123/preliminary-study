# Regulatory Obligation Specification

## Feature
Students practice basic maths numeracy by speaking answers aloud into their device microphone. A speech-recognition component transcribes spoken responses and provides immediate feedback. All responses are logged in an in-app history so students can review previous attempts and replay lessons.

## Applicable Provisions and Obligations

OB1: The application must identify and document a lawful basis under Art. 6 for each distinct processing activity — microphone capture, speech-to-text transcription, automated feedback, and persistent storage of response history — before any processing of student data begins.

OB2: Where the lawful basis for any processing activity is consent (including consent to microphone access and retention of voice data), the application must obtain consent that is freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous, and must provide a mechanism to withdraw consent that is as easy to use as the mechanism to give it.

OB3: Because the application is an information society service directed at students who may be under 16, consent is valid only for students aged 16 or over; for students below that age, consent must be given or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility, and the application must make reasonable efforts to verify that parental authorisation before enabling microphone-based features.

OB4: Voice recordings captured via the microphone constitute biometric personal data; processing of such data is prohibited unless the student — or, where the student is below the applicable age threshold, their parent or guardian — provides explicit consent, or another applicable exception under Art. 9(2) is documented and applied.

OB5: Voice recordings, transcriptions, and response-history logs must be collected solely for the specified purposes of numeracy practice, automated feedback, and lesson-history review, and must not be further processed in any manner incompatible with those purposes.

OB6: The application must capture and retain only data strictly necessary for the stated purposes: transcribed answers and session metadata sufficient for feedback and history review; raw audio recordings must not be retained beyond the duration required to complete transcription.

OB7: Student response logs and session-history records must be retained for no longer than 12 months from the date of each session, after which they must be securely and irreversibly deleted. [retention: 12 months from the date of each session]

OB8: At the point of first microphone use or account registration — whichever is earlier — the application must present each student (and, where required, their parent or guardian) with clear information comprising: the purposes of processing, the legal basis relied upon, any recipients of the data, and the applicable retention period of 12 months. [retention: 12 months from the date of each session]

OB9: The application must provide a mechanism enabling students, or their parents or guardians where applicable, to request erasure of their personal data — including any retained voice recordings, transcriptions, and response-history logs — without undue delay.

OB10: The application must implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures — including encryption of voice data in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and secure storage of response logs — proportionate to the risk associated with processing biometric and educational performance data of students.
